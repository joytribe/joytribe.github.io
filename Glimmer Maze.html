Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026
Android app: io.github.bob.maze · Release 0.12.1

JoyeTribe provides Glimmer Maze. This policy explains how the app handles information when you play, see advertisements, make privacy choices, or contact us.

Your maze progress stays on your device. You do not need an account to play. The app also uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform, which process information for advertising and privacy choices. Offline gameplay does not mean that advertising is free of data collection.

1. Game data stored on your device

Glimmer Maze saves information in the app's local storage so you can continue playing and keep your preferences. This includes:

  • Your current level, position, moves, elapsed play time, explored areas, and recorded walking paths.
  • Tools, opened walls, placed markers, collected light shards, unlocked levels, and completion records.
  • Language, sound levels, control preferences, relaxed mode, reduced animation, and touch feedback settings.
  • The time an app-open advertisement was successfully displayed, used to limit how often ads can appear.

We do not operate an account system, cloud-save service, or backend that receives your maze progress. The app does not provide social profiles, online leaderboards, or chat. Its core maze gameplay can be used offline.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The app includes Google AdMob app-open advertising through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices.

Google's advertising services may process an IP address, which can indicate approximate location; app and ad interactions; performance and diagnostic information; and advertising, app-set, or other device/account identifiers where available. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. What is processed depends on your choices, region, device, and the advertising mode.

Advertising-related information is sent to Google and may be handled by advertising partners involved in delivering an ad. We do not send your saved maze paths or level records as custom advertising data. The app does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

Google describes its practices in its Privacy Policy, advertising privacy information, and Mobile Ads data disclosure. An advertiser's website or app has its own policy if you choose to open it.

3. Consent and privacy choices

At startup, the app asks UMP to update privacy information and presents a privacy message when required and available. It initializes and requests ads only when UMP indicates that ads may be requested. If it cannot obtain that eligibility, it skips advertising and allows access to the game.

When Google requires an ongoing privacy entry point, open the game's Settings → Ad privacy choices to review the available choices. This button is shown when required by UMP. Changing a choice invalidates the app's cached ad so future requests can use the updated state.

You can also review advertising ID and ad privacy controls in Android's system settings. Available controls and menu names vary by Android version and device. These choices do not necessarily remove all ads or all information used for advertising.

4. Device capabilities and permissions

Network access and network-state information support ads and privacy messages. The bundled advertising services declare advertising ID and Android AdServices capabilities for applicable advertising features. They also include wake-lock and foreground-service permissions supplied by the SDK.

The game does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. It does not use these capabilities to play a maze. It does not add background music playback when you leave the app. Local game saving uses app-private storage and does not require access to your personal files.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Your local progress and preferences remain on the device until they are replaced during normal use or you remove the app's data. Updating the app normally preserves them. Glimmer Maze does not offer its own cloud backup or synchronization; the app disables Android app backup and excludes its data from the configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules.

Local storage uses Android's app storage protections. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Google and other advertising services apply their own retention periods and safeguards as described in their policies. Their information may be processed in countries other than your own.

6. Deleting data and exercising choices

To erase the game's local data, open Android Settings → Apps → Glimmer Maze → Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This removes local progress, preferences, and locally stored privacy/ad state and cannot be undone through the game. Uninstalling also removes the app's local data. The game currently has no in-app button that deletes all records.

Deleting local data does not automatically erase information already processed by Google, advertisers, or another service. Use the relevant provider's privacy controls or contact that provider about its information. We cannot recover local progress that has been erased.

You may contact us about privacy questions or requests available to you under applicable law. Because we do not keep a server copy of maze progress, we cannot identify or retrieve that local data remotely. We can explain the app's handling and direct you to relevant provider controls.

7. Information you send to us

If you email JoyeTribe, we receive your email address and the content you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request and address related support or privacy issues. Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and any related record-keeping obligations. Our support email is provided by 163.com; messages are also processed by the email services used to send and receive them under those providers' practices.

8. Children and families

Glimmer Maze is currently configured as a general-audience game, rather than a child-directed advertising service. Child-specific or teen-restricted advertising treatment is not enabled in this release. Parents or guardians who have a concern about a child's information should contact JoyeTribe and may also use Google's privacy controls for information held by Google.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Changes apply as described in the updated policy and, where required, with additional notice or choices.
