PitchLab

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how PitchLab, also named 音感练习室 in Simplified Chinese, handles information. It applies to the Android app with package name io.github.bob.pitchlib. “We” and “us” refer to the PitchLab developer, who can be contacted at joyetribe@163.com.

At a glance

  • Practice plans, answers, and session history are stored on your device.
  • No account, microphone access, or voice recording is required.
  • Online advertising and consent services are provided by Google and may process device and advertising data.
  • You can remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

1. Information stored on your device

PitchLab processes its note recognition and interval exercises locally. It stores the following information in the app’s private storage:

  • Practice plan names and settings, including notes, octave ranges, intervals, note order, and question counts.
  • Practice questions, selected answers, correctness, response times, replay counts, interruptions, session dates, and progress needed to resume a session or show a report.
  • Default settings, language preferences, and other app state.
  • The most recent successful ad display time, used to limit ad frequency, and privacy choices maintained by Google’s consent tools.

We do not operate an app backend that automatically receives your practice plans, answers, or session history. The app does not attach those records or your plan names to advertising requests. Core practice and bundled piano audio work without an internet connection.

PitchLab does not ask for an account, name, phone number, payment details, or a recording of your voice. It plays bundled piano sounds and does not use the microphone.

PitchLab uses Google AdMob app open ads. According to Google’s Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, advertising services may collect and share the following information:

Google uses advertising data to deliver and measure ads, analyze and improve its services, and help prevent fraud. Actual processing depends on privacy choices, device settings, location, and the services used. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Google processes this information under its own policies. See the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, and Google Advertising Technologies information.

3. Consent and advertising choices

PitchLab uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to update consent information and present privacy messages when required. The app checks whether ads may be requested before initializing and requesting ads. Choices made through these forms are handled by Google’s consent tools.

When Google indicates that a privacy options entry is required, you can use Ad privacy options on the home screen or in the tutorial’s privacy section to review your choices. The option may be hidden where it is not required.

Your Android device may also provide controls to reset or delete the advertising ID and manage advertising privacy. Available settings depend on your Android version and device. Changing these settings does not necessarily remove all ads or all processing needed for ad delivery and security.

Core practice does not require an internet connection or account. An unavailable ad does not prevent you from using the practice features.

4. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: support advertising, consent messages, and checks for network availability.
  • Media playback foreground service and wake lock: support audio playback and related service operation.
  • Advertising ID and Android advertising services: may be used by Google’s advertising SDK where supported and permitted.

PitchLab does not request microphone, camera, contacts, precise location, SMS, or broad file-access permissions. General location may still be inferred from the IP address used for advertising, as described above.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Practice records remain in local app storage and are not automatically uploaded or sold by us. Google and its advertising partners may handle advertising information under their own policies and the available privacy choices.

If you open an advertiser’s website or another external service, that service’s privacy policy applies. Android, the app store, and your device manufacturer may independently process installation, security, or diagnostic information under their own policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Practice records and settings remain on your device until they are changed or removed. To remove all local app data, use Android’s app storage settings to clear PitchLab’s storage, or uninstall the app. This removes your local plans, history, and saved progress.

You can also delete an individual practice plan by long-pressing its card and choosing Delete plan. Confirming deletes the plan’s practice history and unfinished progress as well. A minimal local deletion marker remains to prevent delayed saves from restoring removed records; clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes that marker.

PitchLab does not provide cloud synchronization. The app disables Android app backup and excludes its data from Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules.

Data already processed by Google is subject to Google’s retention and deletion practices. Clearing or uninstalling PitchLab does not automatically delete information held by Google. Use Google’s privacy controls or contact Google for requests about data it holds. We cannot remotely delete practice records that exist only on your device.

7. Information you send to us

If you choose to email us, we process your email address and the contents you send to respond to your inquiry and handle your request. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Correspondence is kept only as needed to address the inquiry, maintain necessary support records, or meet applicable obligations. You may contact us about deletion of correspondence. This support contact is separate from the app’s on-device practice storage.

8. Children’s privacy

The app does not provide account registration, public profiles, messaging, or user-content uploads. We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children. Advertising services may process information as described in this policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child’s personal information has been handled improperly in connection with PitchLab, contact us at joyetribe@163.com. Requests concerning information held by Google may also need to be directed to Google.

9. Security and international processing

Practice records are kept in Android’s private app storage. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Google and email service providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their policies and applicable safeguards. PitchLab does not transfer your local practice history to a developer-operated server.

10. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app’s features, advertising services, or information practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when the policy changes.

11. Contact the developer

For questions about this policy, privacy choices, or requests concerning information handled by the PitchLab developer, email joyetribe@163.com. Please identify PitchLab in your message so that we can direct your request correctly.
